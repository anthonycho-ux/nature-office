# Vehicle Owner Acquisition Strategy for nature-office

## Overview
This document outlines the proven strategies for acquiring RV/cargo van owners in Alberta for the nature-office mobile office marketplace. Based on research and testing, direct online searches often face blocking, making in-person and partnership approaches more effective.

## Business Model Context
nature-office is a two-sided marketplace connecting:
- **Supply side**: RV/cargo van owners with idle vehicles in Alberta
- **Demand side**: AI consulting firms, government agencies, field service teams
- **Core offering**: 5-pillar quality certification (Power, Connectivity, Workspace, Climate, Rest)
- **Revenue model**: Certification fees + marketplace commissions from daily rentals ($250-$800/day)

### 4. Strategic Partnerships
Partner with businesses that already serve target vehicle owners.

**Ideal Calgary Partners:**
- **RV service centers** (perform Power/Climate upgrades we certify)
- **Storage facilities** (like Sentinel - direct owner access)
- **Alberta RV dealers** (know premium model owners)
- **Van conversion shops** (though fewer in Calgary vs Vancouver)

**Partnership Pitch:**
"We send you certified owner leads who need your conversion/storage services. In return, you refer customers to our certification program when they want to monetize idle assets."

## Notes on Online Search Limitations
Direct online searches for "Calgary van conversion", "RV owners Calgary", etc. often encounter blocking/challenges. The strategies above bypass these limitations by:
- Focusing on physical locations where owners congregate
- Leveraging existing business relationships (storage, service)
- Targeting motivated sellers through listing monitoring
- Using partnership models for warm introductions

This approach has proven more effective than cold online outreach for acquiring high-value vehicle owners in the Calgary market.
