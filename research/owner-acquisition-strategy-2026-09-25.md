# Research Updates: 2026-09-25

## Topic: Individual Owner Acquisition Strategy for nature-office Marketplace

### Discoveries:
- Direct individual owner acquisition requires a multi-channel approach focused on where idle vehicle owners congregate
- Storage facilities (like Sentinel Storage Calgary) represent the highest concentration of verified idle assets
- Private party listing owners are highly motivated to monetize assets and respond well to alternative income propositions
- Service centers and upfitters have direct access to owners investing in 5-pillar upgrades (solar, batteries, insulation)
- Community engagement in vanlife/RV groups builds trust before monetization discussions
- Fleet owners represent underutilized high-volume opportunities with predictable idle periods

### Impact:
- Shift from broad online search to targeted channel-specific strategies
- Prioritize storage facility partnerships as primary acquisition channel (highest intent, lowest friction)
- Develop channel-specific messaging that speaks to owner motivations (passive income, asset utilization, risk reduction)
- Implement pre-screening qualification to focus efforts on high-probability conversions
- Create automated monitoring for private listings to capture motivated sellers at peak decision moments
- Build partnership incentives for service providers who refer qualified owners

- Successful Lightpanda fetches of Sentinel Storage Calgary pages (verified storage facility accessibility)
- Analysis of AutoTrader Calgary private seller listing patterns
- Research on Alberta RV storage facility locations and contact information
- Investigation of Calgary vanlife community structures on Facebook/Reddit
- Investigation of Calgary service centers specializing in solar/battery/insulation upgrades
- Research on Alberta commercial fleet utilization patterns (seasonal idle periods)
